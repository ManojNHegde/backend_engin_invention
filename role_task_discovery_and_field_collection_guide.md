# Role Task Discovery and Field Collection Guide

**Purpose:** One interview guide for any role: find what the role really does, check each task, and collect what is needed for the task card and the screen fields.

**Status:** Working guide — a method, not approved business rules
**Example used:** Credit Controller, T4 only (Part B)

---

## How to use it

1. **Work first, document second.** Don't show the existing task list until the role holder has described their work.
2. **Their words.** Write answers as they say them. Clean up later.
3. **Real examples.** Ask for the last real case, and look at the record or screen if allowed.
4. **Mark every answer:** **Fact** (a source or a real case) · **Proposal** (our reading) · **Open question** · **Confirmed** (the business owner agreed).
5. **Never** turn a Proposal into a Fact without saying so.

# Part A — For any role

## 1. The words we use

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Responsibility** | The result a group of tasks is for: something the role must keep right and would answer for if it were neglected. An outcome, not an activity. |
| **Task** | A piece of work one role owns that is worth calling done on its own: a clear start and end, one result, and you can see it was done. |
| **Step** | One action inside a task. Not tracked on its own. |
| **Type** | What the task is, read from its *Done when*: Decide, Make, Record, Ask, Chase, Answer, Assess, Report, Carry through, Run a batch (`shared_docs/taxonomy.md`). |

## 2. Opening: find the work

> "I want to understand the work you actually do. I won't assume the current document is right. Tell me in your own words first; then we'll go through each piece of work."

Ask, **before** showing any list:
1. What work comes to you in a normal day or week?
2. What happens only when someone asks, or something unusual happens?
3. What is on your calendar each month or quarter?
4. What do you finish yourself, and what do you send to someone else?
5. What does the system do for you?
6. What do people think you do, but you don't?
7. Walk me through one recent example of each.

- Write each piece of work as a **candidate** (§6).
- Don't ask only "what are your tasks?": people name screens and buttons, not work.

## 3. Responsibilities

Ask:
1. What result are you answerable for?
2. If nobody did this for a month, what would go wrong?
3. Can it go wrong by doing **too much**, as well as too little?
4. How would your manager see that it's being kept?
5. Which pieces of work serve this same result?

- Write it as an **outcome:** "Credit limits stay right", not "Review credit limits".
- Group tasks by the **same why**, never by the same screen.

## 4. Each task: questions → card field

Take one candidate at a time. Start with: **"Tell me about the last time you did this."**

| Ask | Fills |
|---|---|
| What made it start? How often? | Trigger & cadence |
| What must already be in place? | Requires |
| What one thing do you work on: an order, a customer, a change? Can you finish one while another waits? | Subject |
| What do you look at before deciding? | Sees |
| What choices do you have? When is it finished? | Decision · *Done when* |
| Who gets it next? Does your part end when you send it? | Hand-off |
| Who do you tell, even if they don't act? | Sends |
| How do you know it's waiting for you, or for someone else? | Held by |
| What unusual cases come up? What stops you? | Exceptions |
| Which company rule do you check against? Who owns it? | Parameters |
| What can you decide alone? What needs approval? What can you never do? | Rules allowed |
| How would anyone know this is being neglected? | Indicators |
| If nobody did this, what would go wrong? | Serves |

- **Steps are not tasks.** "First I check overdue, then exposure" are steps.
- **Writes** (the record) and **Screen** are not asked: confirm them from the system.

## 5. Each task: what they fill in

Start with: **"Show me the last one. What did you fill in, in what order?"**

For each thing they name, ask:
1. Do you **enter** it, or is it **already there**?
2. Do you type it, pick it, tick it, or attach it?
3. Must you fill it always, only for one choice, or never?
4. What values are allowed? Any limit?
5. What would stop you from sending it?

Record one row per field:

| Field | Entered or shown | Type | Required | Check | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. Is it a task, and what type?

**Task test** (all must pass):

| Test | Question |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Does a clear event start it? |
| Owner | Does this role own finishing it? |
| Result | Does it give one result? |
| End | Can someone see it's done? |
| Independence | Can it be tracked on its own? |
| Boundary | Is it clear against the neighbouring work? |

- **Keep** — several steps, one result.
- **Split** — parts have their own trigger, owner and end.
- **Merge** — two labels, same work and end.
- **Don't decide yet** — owner, trigger or end unclear: write the question.
- A different **screen, button or field** is never a reason to split.

**Type** (from the *Done when*):
- Read the *Done when* and match its ending to a type.
- **Many items, same action** → same type, *many at once*.
- **Fits two types** → check whether it is two tasks.

## 7. After discovery: compare and close

Compare with the existing document:
1. Do they really do this today?
2. Is the trigger, owner and end right?
3. Is it a task, a step, information, or someone else's work?
4. What did the document miss?

Close the interview:
1. What did I misunderstand?
2. What did we miss?
3. Who can confirm what we couldn't settle?

## 8. What you hand in

1. **Responsibilities** — outcomes, with their tasks.
2. **Tasks** — one card each, with its type.
3. **Fields** — one table per task (§5).
4. **Decisions** — keep, split, merge, not a task; with why.
5. **Open questions** — and who answers each.
6. **Changes** — what moved from the existing document, and why.

# Part B — Example: Credit Controller, T4 Review credit limits

*All answers below are **Proposals** from the current analysis, to confirm in the interview.*

## 1. Questions → card

| Ask | Expected answer | Fills |
|---|---|---|
| "What made you start the last review?" | The quarter came round, a limit got too old, or the Sales Manager suggested a customer | Trigger |
| "What must be in place?" | A credit customer with billing history | Requires |
| "What do you decide on: one customer, or all?" | Each customer on its own | Subject |
| "What do you look at?" | Current and suggested limit, on-time %, DSO, order trend, risk band | Sees |
| "What choices do you have?" | Keep, or propose a new limit | Decision |
| "Who gets it next?" | Accounts Executive edits → I check it (T5) → Accounts Manager confirms | Hand-off |
| "Which rule do you check against?" | The company's limit-review period, and the suggested-limit formula | Parameters |
| "Can you skip a review?" | No, the cycle is company policy | Rules allowed |
| "How would anyone know it's neglected?" | Customers past the review period | Indicators |
| "If nobody did this, what goes wrong?" | Limits too high or too low for what the customer can carry | Serves → R2 |

## 2. What they fill in

**Ask:** "Show me the last time you asked for a limit change. What did you fill in, in what order?"

| They say | Field | Entered or shown | Type | Required |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "I select the customer" | Customer | Picked | From the list (several for bulk) | Yes |
| "I see the old limit" | Current limit | Shown | Amount | — |
| "I see what the system suggests" | Suggested limit | Shown | Amount | — |
| "I keep it or change it" | Decision | Entered | Keep · Propose | Yes |
| "I enter the new limit" | New limit | Entered | Amount | Yes, if Propose |
| "I write why" | Reason | Entered | Text | Yes for Propose · Keep: **ask** |
| "From when" (if they say it) | Effective date | Entered | Date | **Ask** |
| "It goes to the Accounts Executive" | Sent to | Shown | Fixed | — |

**Can't send when:** no customer · Propose with no new limit, or the same as now · no reason.

## 3. Task test and type

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Trigger | 🟠 Three triggers (quarter, period lapsed, suggestion): same work, so still one task |
| Owner | ✅ The Credit Controller reviews and recommends |
| Result | ✅ The limit fits what the customer can carry |
| End | ✅ Per customer: kept, or a change requested |
| Independence | 🟠 The unit is **one customer**; the quarterly review is many at once |
| Boundary | ✅ Ends at the Accounts Executive; checking the edit is T5 |

- **Type:** **Decide**, *many at once* — could also be **Ask** (the change is handed over).
- **Verdict:** Keep as one task. Validate.

## 4. Open questions for T4

1. Is the quarterly review one sign-off, or one decision per customer?
2. Does "keep the limit" need a reason?
3. Is "request a change" part of the review, or a separate Ask?
4. Is there an effective date?

---

**Final rule:** Ask what really happens first. Check the document after. Never show a guess as a fact.
